## 1. Why Kynvelo Won

Evaluated against 29 other coined candidates across a 100-point model (legal risk, domain availability, distinctiveness, brandability, memorability, pronunciation clarity, scalability, visual identity), Kynvelo scored **94/100** — the highest of any candidate — and was the only finalist with a completely clean cross-jurisdictional trademark landscape.

| Rank | Name | Score | Legal Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Kynvelo** | **94** | 🟢 GREEN — clean |
| 2 | Alvrix | 89 | 🟢 GREEN — clean |
| 3 | Torvant | 86 | 🟢 GREEN — clean |
| 4 | Fortivio | 82 | 🟡 YELLOW — minor coexistence risk |
| 5 | Kinvero | 79 | 🟡 YELLOW — moderate risk (Kinvera conflict) |

**Why Kynvelo beat the runner-up (Alvrix):**
1. **Acoustic warmth vs. technical austerity** — Alvrix reads well for a developer API but feels cold in consumer-facing, member-touching moments (nutrition logging, streaks, turnstile scans). Kynvelo balances software credibility with athletic vitality.
2. **Global pronunciation ergonomics** — Alvrix ends in a dense consonant cluster (`/vrɪks/`) that creates friction in regional Indian dialects and Romance languages. Kynvelo's consonant-vowel rhythm (C-V-C-C-V-C-V) travels more naturally across markets.
3. **Pristine clearance** — zero identical or phonetically confusing marks across every jurisdiction and class searched.

## 2. Etymology & Meaning

- **Construction:** Greek *kinēsis* (motion/movement) + Latin *velox* (velocity/swift)
- **Brand meaning:** The operating platform for kinetic enterprises — converting physical gym effort and front-desk workflows into high-velocity digital intelligence.
- **Two core ideas it's built on:**
  - **Kinetic Intelligence** — the science of physical effort, human movement, athletic progress.
  - **Velocity of Scale** — the operational acceleration of modern fitness businesses.

## 6. Visual Identity Summary

### Wordmark & symbol
- Built on an 8-point vector grid; diagonal cuts on "K" and "V" suggest forward momentum, solid vertical stems suggest system reliability. +40 tracking for legibility on dashboards and signage.
- **Symbol:** a geometric monogram "K" — open vertical anchor bar + two opposing dynamic chevrons; upper chevron at 45° representing growth.
- **Lockups:** Horizontal (monogram + wordmark, standard for web/nav/documents) and Stacked (monogram above wordmark, for signage/splash screens/apparel).
- **App icon:** Kynvelo "K" in Kinetic Indigo → Neon Volt gradient on Obsidian Slate (#0B0F19), 22% inner safe zone.
- **Monochrome:** solid black for engraving/thermal print; solid white for dark/video overlays; single-color Kinetic Indigo for merchandise/stationery.

> **Note on Color & Design Tokens:** The legacy palette (Obsidian Slate, Kinetic Indigo, Steel Cyan, Neon Volt) has been removed per product requirements. A bespoke, production-grade OKLCH palette and design token architecture will be generated using the Impeccable and UI/UX Pro Max design systems (strictly avoiding generic AI clichés and indigo/purple bans).
